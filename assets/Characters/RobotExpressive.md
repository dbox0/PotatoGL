# RobotExpressive

Animated humanoid for testing skinned meshes and clips (Dance, Death, Idle, Jump, No, Punch,
Running, Sitting, Standing, ThumbsUp, Walking, WalkJump, Wave, Yes).

- Model by Tomás Laulhé (Quaternius), CC0 1.0: https://www.patreon.com/quaternius
- glTF conversion and facial morph targets by Don McCurdy
- Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive
